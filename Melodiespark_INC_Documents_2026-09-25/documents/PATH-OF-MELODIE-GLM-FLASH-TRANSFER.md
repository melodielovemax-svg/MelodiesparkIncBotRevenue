# 18. MUSICAL COMBAT GRAMMAR

## Universal resources

- **Guard/Poise:** resistance to interruption and stagger.

# 19. MAAKARA CONSTELLATION AND PROTOCOL GEMS

## MAAKARA Constellation

The passive network should create legible routes between broad archetypes while rewarding cross-domain discoveries. Node families include attributes, weapon technique, damage conversion, ailments, minions/constructs, guard, evasion, ward, resource engines, item interaction, and Pillar-aligned keystones.

Keystones must fundamentally alter rules and state their downside. Examples:

- **Perfect Silence:** Sonic damage becomes Void damage; you cannot generate Tempo from repeated skills.
- **Shared Pulse:** a percentage of your recovery affects linked allies; your personal recovery is reduced.
- **Golden Dissonance:** Dissonance no longer damages you but drains Resonance while above its safe threshold.
- **Last Refrain:** lethal damage consumes all Tempo to prevent defeat; long internal cooldown.

## Protocol Gems

Active Protocol Gems define actions. Modifier Protocols change delivery, cost, behavior, or scaling. Links are limited by equipment architecture and item investment. Modifiers must declare compatibility through tags rather than hand-coded name checks.

Example chain:

```text
Echo Lance [Projectile, Sonic, Spell]
  + Splinter Protocol [adds projectiles, lowers single-hit power]
  + Returning Phrase [projectiles return]
  + Dissonant Edge [converts part of Sonic to Void]
  + Crescendo Trigger [releases a pulse after repeated hits]
```

Validation must prevent recursive trigger loops, zero-cost infinite casts, integer overflow, unsafe projectile counts, and combinations that destroy frame-time budgets.

Settlements evolve through player decisions. Visible changes may include architecture, NPC population, vendor inventories, ambient music, defense events, and travel access. Choices should create alternate advantages rather than disguising one objectively correct selection.

# 24. REALM CONSTELLATION AND ENDGAME

After Act X, players access the **Realm Constellation**, a navigable network of procedural and authored realms. Each node declares biome, objective, Protocol modifiers, enemy families, boss chance, reward focus, difficulty, instability, and adjacency effects.

## Infinite Protocol loop

1. Choose or craft a realm key.
2. Review modifiers and reward promise.
3. Enter a bounded procedural realm.
4. Complete its objective before failure conditions.
5. Defeat guardian or extract.
6. Improve the Constellation and target future rewards.
7. Challenge Sovereign and Omega sequences.

Endgame objectives include elimination, survival, escort, ritual interruption, defense, pursuit, corruption cleansing, split-path choice, and boss rush. Avoid endlessly reskinning the same kill counter.

The Constellation needs pity systems, target farming, bad-luck protection, disconnect recovery, deterministic seeds for defect reproduction, and seasonal migration policy.

# 25. SOCIAL, CO-OP, PVP, AND TRADE BOUNDARIES

The release baseline should remain fully playable solo and offline-capable where platform integrations are unavailable. Online systems are layered features, not dependencies for basic launch.

## Cooperative play target

- party size: initially 1-4;
- join/leave rules per activity;
- personal or explicitly allocated loot;
- enemy scaling that avoids health-sponge multiplication;
- revive limits and boss reset rules;
- latency-tolerant input presentation with server-authoritative outcomes where online;
- ping wheel, text options, mute/block/report, and privacy controls.

Optional PvP requires separate balance coefficients and rewards that do not force PvE players to participate. Never use PvP tuning to silently damage PvE build identity.

A player market needs fraud controls, searchable affixes, price history boundaries, rate limits, binding rules, seasonal separation, dispute/support tools, and no implication that fictional MA is real cryptocurrency.

# 26. UI, UX, ONBOARDING, AND ACCESSIBILITY

## Required screens

Boot diagnostics, legal/accessibility first-run, title, profile/character selection, Origin selection, settings, HUD, inventory, equipment, Constellation, Protocol Gem linking, crafting, stash, map/Realm Constellation, journal, codex, social, death recap, pause, support diagnostics, credits, and safe quit.

## First-hour onboarding

- minute 0-5: movement, camera, primary attack, first musical response;
- minute 5-15: active skill, dodge/mobility, first elite telegraph;
- minute 15-30: loot comparison, equip, one Modifier Protocol;
- minute 30-45: crafting introduction and meaningful choice;
- minute 45-60: first lieutenant with a readable multi-phase mechanic.

Teach through play, contextual hints, and a replayable codex. Every tutorial can be dismissed, reviewed later, and adapted for controller or keyboard/mouse.

## Accessibility baseline

- remappable controls with conflict detection;
- keyboard/mouse and controller parity;
- scalable UI and subtitles;
- color-blind-safe telegraphs plus non-color signals;
- photosensitivity controls;
- camera shake and motion blur controls;
- audio dynamic-range and independent channel controls;
- timing-window assistance;
- aim and targeting assistance options;
- hold/toggle alternatives;
- readable font option;
- subtitle speaker labels and direction where useful.

# 27. CAMERA, CONTROLS, AND TARGETING

Use an elevated isometric camera with dynamic combat zoom and authored boss framing. Camera motion must never conceal lethal information. Occluding geometry requires fade, cutaway, or camera-aware material handling.

Input actions require stable semantic names independent of device. Provide input buffering, aim mode policy, target selection, ground targeting, movement-skill direction, interact priority, loot priority, and UI focus recovery. Controller inventory and skill linking must be designed directly, not treated as mouse emulation.

# 28. AUDIO SYSTEM AS GAMEPLAY INFRASTRUCTURE

Implement adaptive music as layered stems driven by encounter intensity, biome identity, boss phase, player Tempo, and narrative state. Preserve musical continuity across transitions where practical.

Audio priority classes must prevent ambient density from masking telegraphs. Critical cues need concurrency protection and visual/haptic equivalents. Define loudness targets, platform output modes, dynamic-range presets, streamer-safe music policy, voice localization hooks, and automated missing-asset checks.

# 29. TECHNICAL GAME ARCHITECTURE FOR UE 5.8

## Recommended Unreal systems

- Gameplay Ability System for abilities, effects, tags, attributes, and authority boundaries.
- Enhanced Input for device-independent mappings and contexts.
- Niagara for budgeted combat effects.
- Chaos for selective gameplay physics, not uncontrolled decoration.
- Motion Warping and Control Rig for responsive authored combat motion.
- Data Assets/Data Tables for tunable gameplay definitions with validation.
- Asset Manager and Primary Asset Labels for deterministic cook inclusion.
- World Partition for large realms where it materially helps.
- Mass AI only for suitable crowds; core combatants need appropriate full behavior.
- Nanite, Lumen, and Virtual Shadow Maps only after target-hardware measurement.

## Module boundaries

Prefer modules/plugins for Core, Combat, Abilities, Items, Progression, AI, BossProtocols, Quests, World, UI, Audio, Save, Telemetry, Online, EditorTools, and Tests. Dependencies should point inward toward stable shared contracts. Editor-only code must not leak into Shipping.

## Data validation

Add editor/commandlet validation for duplicate IDs, missing references, cyclic triggers, invalid tags, absent icons, missing localization keys, unreachable loot tiers, incompatible elite modifiers, uncooked primary assets, invalid quest prerequisites, and boss phases without exits.

# 30. SAVE, ACCOUNT, ENTITLEMENT, AND MIGRATION

The save format needs schema version, game build, platform/account identity where available, character records, campaign flags, inventory/stash, Constellation state, settings, discovered lore, and integrity metadata. Never place credentials or private keys in saves.

Requirements:

- atomic writes and backup rotation;
- corruption detection and recovery;
- migration tests from every supported schema;
- offline-to-online conflict policy;
- cloud-save conflict UX if enabled;
- character deletion confirmation and recovery window;
- seasonal-to-permanent migration rules;
- deterministic support export with sensitive fields removed.

Entitlement failure must not erase local progress. Error messages should distinguish offline service, invalid ownership, expired session, maintenance, and corrupted installation.

# 31. CONTENT FACTORY: FROM BRIEF TO REAL ASSET

The existing 30,000 `BRIEF_READY` records are planning inventory, not playable content. Use an explicit lifecycle:

```text
IDEA -> BRIEF_READY -> SOURCE_ACQUIRED -> LICENSE_VERIFIED -> IMPORTED -> VALIDATED -> INTEGRATED -> TESTED -> APPROVED -> COOKED -> SHIPPED
```

Each asset requires stable ID, category, source/license, owner, intended map/system, technical budgets, scale, collision, LOD/Nanite policy, materials, sockets, animation needs, audio dependencies, localization, accessibility notes, validation result, and cook status.

Prioritize a **Minimum Complete Asset Set** for one vertical slice before mass production: one hero kit, one settlement, one field biome, one dungeon, five monster families, three elites, one lieutenant, one complete Maakara boss, full core UI, combat VFX, adaptive audio, loot set, and release branding.

# 32. PERFORMANCE AND HARDWARE BUDGETS

The known development machine has historically been a Dell OptiPlex 7040 with i5-6500, GTX 1050 2 GB, and upgraded RAM. Reconfirm current hardware. Do not enable expensive UE features by prestige; measure them.

Establish tiers for minimum, recommended, and development hardware. For each, define resolution, frame-rate target, CPU frame budget, GPU frame budget, VRAM ceiling, system memory ceiling, load-time target, shader-stutter policy, actor/AI counts, Niagara budgets, texture pool, shadow policy, and scalable fallback.

The first optimization target is stable play and readable combat, not maximum graphical switches. Capture Unreal Insights traces and representative scene profiles before and after material changes.

# 33. TELEMETRY, PRIVACY, AND PLAYER SAFETY

Telemetry must be purposeful, documented, consent-aware where required, and free of secrets. Useful events include first-run completion, tutorial steps, deaths, boss attempts, skill usage, build changes, crashes, load failures, progression stalls, and accessibility option usage in aggregate.

Every event needs schema version, build version, session correlation, timestamp policy, required/optional fields, retention, sampling, privacy classification, and owner. Provide offline queues with bounded storage and safe failure behavior.

Online communities require moderation, mute/block/report, naming rules, evidence retention boundaries, abuse escalation, parental considerations, and transparent enforcement. Dark patterns, deceptive scarcity, and pay-to-win pressure are outside the product promise.

# 34. MONETIZATION AND LIVE-OPERATIONS ETHICS

Initial monetization should favor a clear premium product or transparent edition structure. If cosmetics, expansions, or seasonal support are added:

- no purchased combat power;
- no paid random rewards without explicit legal review;
- no disguised real-money value for fictional MA;
- clear prices and contents;
- durable purchase restoration;
- accessible previews;
- no manipulative countdowns;
- age-appropriate protections;
- refund/support paths;
- content production must not compromise the complete base game.

Seasonal Protocols should introduce a fresh mechanic, narrative question, build opportunities, encounter variants, reward family, and end-of-season migration plan. Define cadence only after measuring the team's sustainable throughput.

# 35. LOCALIZATION AND CULTURAL QUALITY

Separate player-facing text from code. Support pluralization, gender/context metadata, variable ordering, font glyph coverage, line expansion, subtitle timing, and controller glyph substitution. Pseudolocalization and missing-key tests should run before content lock.

The Karma Pillars draw from culturally meaningful concepts. Use respectful research and expert review; avoid flattening living traditions into exotic decoration. Original worldbuilding must be clear about fictional interpretation.

# 36. TEST STRATEGY

## Automated layers

- unit tests for formulas, tags, loot, crafting, quest transitions, save migrations, and protocol compatibility;
- integration tests for abilities, equipment, bosses, entitlement, and persistence;
- Unreal automation tests for maps, assets, cook references, and functional gameplay;
- deterministic encounter simulations for balance regression;
- performance baselines for representative scenes;
- packaging and clean-launch smoke tests;
- security and secret scans;
- Golden Path end-to-end evidence.

## Manual quality passes

Combat feel, readability, controller UX, accessibility, narrative flow, audio mix, visual hierarchy, exploit discovery, long-session stability, clean-machine install, and recovery from interrupted operations.

Every fixed defect should gain the cheapest reliable regression test. Quarantine is allowed only with an owner, reason, risk, and expiry date.

# 37. VERTICAL SLICE: MINIMUM COMPLETE GAME PROOF

The preferred first complete slice contains:

- one Origin fully polished, with other Origins selectable only if honest content exists;
- one evolving settlement;
- one outdoor region and one dungeon;
- five distinct monster families;
- three elite modifier combinations;
- one lieutenant and one full Maakara Protocol boss;
- ten to fifteen active skills plus meaningful modifiers;
- representative Constellation branch;
- complete loot, crafting, inventory, stash, quest, save/load, death/retry, settings, accessibility, audio, and onboarding loops;
- Win64 Shipping package and clean launch;
- 45-90 minutes of polished first-play content;
- measurable path to replay through builds and difficulty.

This slice is the factory proof. Scale to ten acts and Infinite Protocols only after this loop is fun, stable, measurable, and reproducible.

# 38. CONTENT AND PRODUCTION ESTIMATION

For every feature, track: design status, implementation status, dependency, responsible owner, evidence, performance cost, localization cost, accessibility impact, test coverage, risk, and cut/ship decision.

Use four scope classes:

- **Release Critical:** required for a coherent purchasable game.
- **Vertical Slice Critical:** required to prove the game and factory.
- **Expansion:** valuable after core quality is proven.
- **Experiment:** isolated, feature-flagged, removable.

Large numeric goals such as 30,000 assets or hundreds of scripts are capacity indicators, not quality metrics. Prioritize complete player loops, authored cohesion, and verified integration.
