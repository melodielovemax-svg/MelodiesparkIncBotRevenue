1\. THE PREMISE

The ancient MAAKARA Protocol was created to convert verified human
action into civilization-scale power.

It failed.

Not because the protocol was weak, but because humanity corrupted its
meaning.

Generations later, the world of Melodia exists as a fractured continent
where Karma has become physical energy. Mountains remember murder.
Forests reward sacrifice. Cities are governed by ancient
smart-contract-like relics called Protocols.

At the center of the continent stands the shattered MAAKARA Spire.

Its twelve Karma Pillars once maintained balance.

Now each Pillar has been seized by a corrupted sovereign.

You are a Wayfarer: a resurrected human whose soul contains a fragment
of the Genesis Protocol.

Your mission is not simply to kill monsters.

You must decide what civilization deserves to exist after them.

Skills are called Protocol Gems.

Every active ability can be modified using linked Modifier Protocols.

Kills generate temporary Karma charges.

Consumes player health to massively amplify damage.

## Karma and Crypto Boundary

KPP remains the non-transferable reputation/progression layer.

Three economies must remain separated:

1.  **Game economy:** gold/materials/equipment/trading.
2.  **Karma economy:** KPP, non-transferable reputation, alignment and
    progression.
3.  **Optional crypto reward layer:** separately funded, audited,
    explicitly claimed on-chain rewards.

A secure future reward path is:

`Server-verified gameplay event → Karma Engine → KPP → eligibility policy → anti-cheat/rate limits → explicit player claim → external wallet signature → on-chain transaction → confirmation → audit ledger`

KPP must not simply be assigned a fake cash exchange rate. A client-side
`+KPP` event must never directly create a cryptocurrency balance.

Production crypto rewards remain disabled until separately implemented,
tested, secured, audited and legally reviewed.

Recommended defaults: - `ENABLE_KPP=true` -
`ENABLE_REWARD_ELIGIBILITY=true` - `ENABLE_CRYPTO_REWARDS=false` -
`ENABLE_AUTOMATIC_PAYOUTS=false` - `REQUIRE_PLAYER_CLAIM=true` -
`REQUIRE_EXTERNAL_SIGNER=true`

No private keys or seed phrases should be embedded in the game or Docker
images. No automatic transfers. External wallet approval/signing is
required.
