Rules:
- Verify Stripe signatures against the raw request body.
- Deduplicate by Stripe event ID.
- A success-page redirect never proves payment.
- Order states: DRAFT, CHECKOUT_CREATED, PAYMENT_PENDING, PAID,
  FULFILMENT_PENDING, FULFILLED, FAILED, CANCELLED, REFUNDED, DISPUTED.
- Count commercial revenue only from verified live succeeded payments.
- Keep gross, tax, discount, refund, dispute, fee, and net fields separate.
- Never label Stripe balance as bank balance.
