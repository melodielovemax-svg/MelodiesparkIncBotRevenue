## Positioning

Melodiespark Inc. builds AI-native workflow, developer, automation, and digital-commerce infrastructure for founders, developers, and growing teams.

Primary promise:
“Turn an approved business objective into a governed workflow, a finished deliverable, and evidence you can review.”

Do not claim automatic earnings, guaranteed results, real customers, verified savings, funds under management, financial services, legal advice, token appreciation, cryptocurrency mining, or live revenue unless a real connected source proves the exact claim.

Use status language honestly:
- Available: a product/service that is configured and purchasable.
- In development: planned capability, no purchase CTA.
- Contact sales: service requiring a scoped proposal.
- Waitlist: interest capture only.

## Brand system

- Background: obsidian #0B0F14.
- Primary accent: luminous gold #F5A524.
- Secondary accents: electric cyan #22D3EE and violet #7C3AED.
- Text: white and high-contrast cool gray.
- Typography: clean modern sans-serif for UI; mono style for technical labels.
- Visual language: premium enterprise, future-facing, clean cards, subtle glow, precise grid, no generic crypto imagery, no invented partner logos.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, labeled forms, reduced-motion support.

## Security & Governance

Headline: “Automation with boundaries.”

Explain consent-first data handling, role-based access, approval gates, audit logs, evidence bundles, browser-safe secret handling, human approval for external/financial/contract/transfer actions, provider-based payment confirmation, and explicit user confirmation for Web3/wallet actions.

Add status table:
- VERIFIED: backed by connected evidence.
- DRAFT: planned or prepared, not active.
- PENDING_APPROVAL: awaiting authorized review.
- BLOCKED: required condition missing.

## Contact / Discovery

- Create accurate title/meta/OG/canonical/structured data.
- Ensure responsive layouts and real loading, empty, error, success states.
