## Economy Rule

The former 5,000-entry currency ladder is compressed into **79 canonical currencies**.

- #1 remains **MAAKARA Prime Crown of Genesis** — 6× rarer than the Mirror benchmark.
- #20 remains **Genesis Mirror of the Infinite Protocol** — exactly Mirror-equivalent rarity.
- #1–#20 are preserved exactly from the original apex ladder.
- #21–#79 represent progressively broader bands of the former #21–#5000 economy.
- The 4,921 removed currency identities should not become separate drops. Their old crafting functions can be absorbed as recipes, modifiers, sub-effects, fragments, encounter rewards, or crafting outcomes of the 79 canonical currencies.
- KPP remains separate and non-transferable. In-game MA remains separate from any real-world cryptocurrency.
