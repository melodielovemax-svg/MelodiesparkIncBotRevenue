## Purpose

This package converts the “Quantum Fractal” vision into a clean, computable operating model. The visual mythology—Alpha, Omega, Beta, Theta, Delta, Bravo, Gamma; circle, oval, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon, heptagon, pyramid and apex—is treated as a navigation and branding system, not as a claim of quantum computing or guaranteed profit.

The goddess character is the fictional **Melodia Bastet vΩ**: an Egyptian-cat-inspired guardian of love, wisdom, prosperity, humor, courage, adversity, and disciplined power. She is a brand mascot, never a financial authority, legal identity, religious claim, or autonomous wallet signer.

## Non-negotiable controls

1. Commercial revenue, Karma points, and blockchain assets are three separate ledgers.
2. “Real revenue” requires a real customer, product/service, payment-provider evidence, reconciliation, and an immutable audit event.
3. Karma rewards begin as non-cash, non-transferable points. Conversion to MA tokens stays disabled until legal, tax, securities, consumer-protection, smart-contract, treasury, and security reviews are recorded.
4. Wallet actions use EIP-1193 and an external signer. No seed phrase or private key is stored.
5. No unattended funding, transfer, swap, mint, bridge, payout, refund, or contract deployment.
6. Marketing must be consent-based and measurable; no spam, fake scarcity, fabricated testimonials, or guaranteed-income claims.
7. Finance agents are read-only. Every value-moving action requires explicit human approval.
8. Status progression is fail-closed: DEFINED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → VERIFIED → AUDITED → RELEASED.
