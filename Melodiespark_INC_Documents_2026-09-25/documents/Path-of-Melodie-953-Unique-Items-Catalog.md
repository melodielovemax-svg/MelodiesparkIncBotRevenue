# Path of Melodie: MAAKARA Protocols — 953 Unique Items

## Distribution

Nine rarity levels contain at least 100 uniques each. Because 9 × 100 = 900, the remaining 53 items are assigned to **Mythological Diamond**, producing exactly **953** total.

| Level | Rarity | Unique Items |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Bronze | 100 |
| 2 | Silver | 100 |
| 3 | Gold | 100 |
| 4 | Platinum | 100 |
| 5 | Diamond | 100 |
| 6 | Legendary Diamond | 100 |
| 7 | Mythic Diamond | 100 |
| 8 | Divine Diamond | 100 |
| 9 | Mythological Diamond | 153 |

## Design Rules

- Every item has a stable `POM-U-####` ID and deterministic seed.
- Every unique has an item type, Karma Pillar affinity, theme, signature mechanic, limitation, Protocol-slot count and Fractal/Corruption eligibility.
- All numerical power multipliers are design targets only: `DEFINED / NOT TESTED` until gameplay balance testing.
- The final 53 are apex Mythological Diamond uniques, not a tenth rarity.
