# MELODIESPARK INC. — TITANESQUE 500-POINT A–Z ENTERPRISE BOTREVENUE RELEASE GRID

## Purpose

This master framework defines 500 bounded build, verification, governance, and release requirements for a serious Melodiespark Inc. business-enterprise ecosystem. It covers corporate automation, products and services, sales, BotRevenue, fintech, good-deed services, Karma Power Points, MA Tokens, wallets, portfolio visibility, compliant swaps, quality, and release authority.

This is an implementation and governance template—not evidence that any system, corporation status, customer, payment, revenue, token, liquidity venue, wallet balance, swap, return, or deployment currently exists. Every status starts as `DEFINED` or `UNVERIFIED` until direct evidence proves a higher state.

## Non-negotiable economic boundaries

| Economy | What belongs here | What must never happen |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Real products, real customers, captured fiat or crypto payments, refunds, fees, taxes, disputes, settlements | Never count leads, points, test funds, token issuance, wallet balances, or promises as revenue |
| Karma | Verified good deeds and transparent non-cash reputation points | Never call Karma revenue, cash, an investment, guaranteed value, or automatically redeemable money |
| MA Token | Legally reviewed token utility, issuance, transfers, treasury, contracts, and holder disclosures | Never mint, promise appreciation, market as an investment, or convert points without legal and technical release gates |
| External crypto | User-owned assets observed through read-only wallet and exchange adapters | Never take custody, sign, swap, trade, stake, or transfer without consent, suitability controls where applicable, and explicit per-transaction approval |

**Verified revenue invariant:** `real customer + real product/service + captured payment + authoritative provider evidence + immutable audit record = verified commercial revenue`.

**Good-deed conversion rule:** a verified beneficial action may create Karma under published rules. Any optional conversion from Karma to MA Tokens requires eligibility, anti-fraud review, legal approval, token availability, disclosed rate and fees, user consent, and explicit issuance approval. Any MA Token swap requires a supported lawful venue, real liquidity, price and slippage disclosure, wallet/network verification, and external user signing. No stage is guaranteed.

## Status vocabulary

`UNVERIFIED → DEFINED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → VERIFIED → AUDITED → RELEASED`. No row may skip evidence gates. A document alone proves only `DEFINED`; code alone proves only `IMPLEMENTED`.

## Portfolio and swap guardrails

- Portfolio values are read-only estimates with source, timestamp, currency, and uncertainty; they are not revenue or guaranteed realizable value.
- Wallet connections use public addresses and external signing. Private keys and seed phrases are never requested, stored, logged, or transmitted.
- Each transaction shows the exact network, token contract, decimals, amount, destination, quoted price, price source, fees, gas, slippage, expiry, and risks.
- The user compares the first six and last six destination-address characters and gives fresh approval after the final quote.
- Agents may prepare unsigned proposals but may not autonomously sign, broadcast, swap, transfer, stake, borrow, lend, or trade.
