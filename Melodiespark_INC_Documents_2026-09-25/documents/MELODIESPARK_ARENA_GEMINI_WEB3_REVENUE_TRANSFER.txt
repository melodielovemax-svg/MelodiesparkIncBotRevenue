======================================================================
0. NON-NEGOTIABLE GLOBAL RULES
======================================================================

1. NEVER fabricate:
   - customers
   - revenue
   - payments
   - wallet balances
   - transaction hashes
   - bank connections
   - blockchain confirmations
   - contracts
   - deployments
   - approvals
   - compliance status

2. Evidence rule:
   REAL CUSTOMER
   + REAL PRODUCT
   + REAL PAYMENT
   + VERIFIED EVIDENCE
   + AUDIT RECORD
   = VERIFIED REVENUE

3. Any missing or inconclusive evidence MUST remain:
   UNVERIFIED / PENDING / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED

4. Financial agents are READ-ONLY by default.
   Do not give autonomous agents authority to sign, broadcast, transfer, swap,
   withdraw, bridge, or move funds.

5. All consequential Web3 or treasury actions require:
   - explicit human approval
   - external wallet/signer
   - clear transaction preview
   - network + chain ID verification
   - destination verification
   - amount verification
   - fee preview
   - final user signature

6. Never store or request private keys or seed phrases.
   External wallet signing only.

7. Bank integration must remain DISABLED until a supported provider is connected
   through a legitimate, authorized integration.

8. Crypto off-ramp or fiat withdrawal workflows must be tracked and reconciled,
   not silently or automatically initiated.

9. Preserve user credentials and .env files.
   Never overwrite existing secrets.

10. Every automated workflow must be idempotent, logged, auditable, and fail-closed.
