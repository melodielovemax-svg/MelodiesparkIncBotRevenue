## Stripe alternatives and verification reality

There is **no compliant card, bank-transfer, wallet, marketplace, or fiat payout processor** that can promise “no KYC/KYB” for a Canadian corporation. Merchant verification can occur at onboarding or when activity, payout, chargeback, or risk thresholds are reached. Avoid any provider marketed as a way to bypass identity checks.

| Route | Merchant KYC with a processor | What it enables | Main trade-off |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Stripe, PayPal, Square, Shopify Payments, Adyen, Braintree, Helcim, Moneris | Required / may be required | Fiat cards, wallets, receipts, chargebacks, payouts | Verification is a compliance obligation, not an optional feature. |
| Self-hosted [BTCPay Server](https://btcpayserver.org/) | No processor onboarding | Direct Bitcoin and Lightning payments to a wallet you control | You operate custody, pricing, accounting, refunds, tax records, security, and compliance. |
| Direct self-custody USDC checkout | No payment-processor onboarding | Wallet-to-wallet crypto payments | Network selection, gas, wallet UX, volatility/depeg risk, refunds, sanctions considerations, accounting, and tax responsibilities remain yours. |
| Manual bank transfer / invoice | Your bank verifies the business | Direct CAD settlement through your bank | Not an online card checkout; reconciliation and fraud controls are your responsibility. |

### Recommended architecture for Melodiespark Inc

Use Stripe as the fiat primary rail, while offering **optional self-custody crypto checkout** as a separate, clearly labelled payment method. Keep distinct order records and require explicit human approval for every treasury movement, consistent with the existing treasury policy. Do not represent crypto acceptance as anonymous, tax-free, or a workaround for legal obligations.
