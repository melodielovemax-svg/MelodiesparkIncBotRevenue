## 1. Executive purpose

This document translates ChatGPT Plus capabilities into an actionable operating system for Melodiespark Inc. It distinguishes what AI can generate from what the company must connect, test, verify, approve, deploy, and audit.

The controlling rule is:

> **Generated is not implemented. Implemented is not tested. Tested is not verified. Verified is not released. Released is not automatically revenue.**

Real commercial revenue exists only when all required evidence is present:

> **Real customer + real product or service + real payment + verified evidence + audit record = verified commercial revenue.**

The three economies remain separate:

| Economy | Meaning | Valid evidence | Never treat as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Customer payments and earned business income | Processor record, invoice, settlement, bank record, audit ID | Rewards, points, projections |
| Karma | Reputation, participation, goodwill, leaderboard or internal utility | Rules, event ledger, participant record | Cash, revenue, withdrawable crypto |
| Blockchain | On-chain assets controlled by an authorized wallet | Chain ID, address, contract, RPC result, explorer record | Bank balance or customer revenue without provenance |

## 13. BotRevenue evidence-controlled pipeline

1. Define a real product or service with an owner, price, terms and delivery method.
2. Publish an authorized offer through a real channel.
3. Capture a consenting, identifiable customer record.
4. Create a processor-backed checkout or invoice.
5. Receive a real payment in test mode only during integration testing; label it TEST.
6. Move to live mode only after approval and security validation.
7. Verify webhook authenticity and make processing idempotent.
8. Record payment, fees, taxes, refunds and settlement separately.
9. Reconcile processor settlement to the authorized business account.
10. Create a tamper-evident audit record.
11. Recognize revenue only under the applicable accounting policy.
12. Report verified revenue separately from pipeline, forecasts, rewards and assets.

## 20. Security and privacy requirements

1. Never overwrite `.env` files or expose credentials.
2. Never store private keys, seed phrases or raw authentication tokens in generated reports.
3. Mask sensitive values in logs and examples.
4. Use least privilege, deny-first policies and explicit scopes.
5. Separate development, test, staging and production.
6. Require approval for deployment, publishing, transfers and destructive operations.
7. Validate dependencies and generate a software bill of materials where appropriate.
8. Include rollback, incident response and credential-rotation procedures.
9. Treat uploaded information according to its legal and business sensitivity.
10. Preserve immutable evidence without exposing confidential content.
