1. NON-NEGOTIABLE ENGINEERING LAWS
======================================================================

1. Inspect before modifying.
2. Preserve existing working implementation.
3. Never fabricate customers, users, traffic, demand, revenue, balances, transactions, hashes, deployments, test results, audits, certifications or verification.
4. Synthetic/test/demo/forecast data must be explicitly classified.
5. Never overwrite .env or credentials.
6. Never print or commit secrets.
7. Never request, persist or expose seed phrases/private keys.
8. Web3 financial agents are read-only by default.
9. Mainnet transaction signing requires an external signer and explicit human approval.
10. No autonomous auto-funding or silent fund transfer.
11. Destructive migration requires backup, dry-run, reconciliation and rollback.
12. Every consequential action emits an audit event.
13. Every release claim must be evidence-derived.
14. Prefer one canonical implementation over duplicate projects.
15. Do not replace real integrations with mocks and then call them production-ready.
16. Keep commercial, Karma and blockchain economies logically and financially separated.
17. Karma is reputation/contribution data, not automatically money.
18. Token/reward features remain gated by legal, compliance, treasury and technical approval.
19. Fail closed on ambiguous financial state.
20. Report PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT-RUN truthfully.
