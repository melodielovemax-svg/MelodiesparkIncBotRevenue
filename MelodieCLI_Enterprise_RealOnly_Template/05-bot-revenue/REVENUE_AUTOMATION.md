# Revenue Automation

## Legitimate Pipeline

Offering
→ Lead
→ Qualification
→ Proposal
→ Approval
→ Contract
→ Payment Request
→ Payment Provider
→ Webhook
→ Ledger
→ Reconciliation
→ Fulfillment
→ Customer Receipt

## Never Fabricate

- leads
- customers
- orders
- payments
- revenue
- balances
- transaction hashes

## Worker Model

Workers are logical jobs managed by a bounded scheduler.

Large worker targets do not authorize uncontrolled process creation.
