# No Synthetic Business Data

Synthetic data must never be represented as real business evidence.

Forbidden as production evidence:

- fake customers
- fake payments
- fake orders
- fabricated revenue
- fabricated balances
- fabricated blockchain transactions
- fabricated transaction hashes
- fabricated receipts
- fabricated market demand
- fabricated reviews
- fabricated partnerships

Test data is permitted only when explicitly isolated and labeled TEST DATA.
