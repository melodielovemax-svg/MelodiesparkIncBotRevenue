Operational honesty

Legal & safety boundary

These are engineering notices, not legal advice or evidence of a license.

No license claim

Software cannot create a corporation, license, MSB registration, securities approval, or bank authorization. Verify registrations and obtain qualified Canadian counsel before operating regulated activities.

No guaranteed revenue

Dashboards show recorded events only. Demo data is labeled. No trading strategy, engagement score, or marketing automation guarantees income.

Wallet consent

Private keys are never requested. A wallet signature proves control or authorizes a defined message; it does not bypass KYC, AML, sanctions screening, tax, or provider controls.

Privacy

Do not place residential addresses, bank account numbers, identity documents, live keys, or passwords in this repository. Use a secrets manager and a proper identity system.

Data handling

Implement retention, consent, access, deletion, and breach procedures for PIPEDA/GDPR where applicable. This local MVP is not a compliance certification.

Token rewards

Do not publish an airdrop, faucet, or Karma conversion program until legal classification, funding, sanctions controls, tax treatment, smart-contract audit, and regional availability are complete.
